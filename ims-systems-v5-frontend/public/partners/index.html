How this forecast is calculated
  1. Your maximum. Your client base × the share you expect to move, rounded to whole customers. The forecast never goes above this.
  2. Customers moving across. From month 1, the number you enter moves across each month. In the last month only the remainder joins, so the total stops exactly at your maximum.
  3. Customers leaving. Each month the percentage you choose leaves, counted in whole customers. Part-customers carry over to the next month so the long-run rate stays accurate.
  4. Your revenue share. For each month: customers that month × £150 × 20% (£30 per customer). New customers count from the month they join.
  5. Totals. Year totals add up the 12 months in each year. The 3-year total is Year 1 + Year 2 + Year 3.

Switch the chart to Table to see every month: customers joining, leaving and the total, and your revenue share.

The story

How to talk about iMS OS

Use the same core message everywhere. Pick the pitch length that fits the moment.

One line

iMS OS is the operating system for compliance: every standard, audit, risk, policy and action in one connected platform.

30 seconds

Microsoft runs your productivity. Your CRM runs your sales. Your finance system runs the numbers. But compliance usually lives in spreadsheets, shared drives and email chains. iMS OS gives it an operating system of its own: ISO and British Standards, audits, risk, document control, actions, governance and ESG in one platform, with Alice, an AI compliance co-pilot, built in. Whether you manage one standard or twenty, you get one source of truth and you're audit-ready every day.

2 minutes

Most organisations run compliance across disconnected tools: a risk register in one spreadsheet, corrective actions in another, procedures on a shared drive and audit evidence buried in email. That creates duplicate work, stressful audit preparation and blind spots management can't see.

iMS OS replaces that with one compliance operating system. You map requirements once and track them against every standard you hold, including ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 27001, 22301, 50001, British Standards, PAS, sector schemes such as SIA ACS, and your own internal frameworks. Audits, risks and opportunities, documents, non-conformities and corrective actions all live in the same place and stay connected, so an audit finding raises an action, the action updates the risk, and leadership sees it on a live dashboard.

You can also give auditors, clients and boards secure, read-only, time-limited access, so remote audits and due diligence take minutes instead of weeks. And Alice, the built-in AI co-pilot, answers questions on any clause, finds evidence and spots trends.

With ISO 9001:2026 and ISO 14001:2026 now published, every certified organisation has a transition ahead of it. That makes now the right time to move compliance onto one platform.

Handling objections

We already use spreadsheets and they work.

They work until audit week or until someone leaves. iMS OS keeps the same information but connects it, assigns owners and keeps evidence ready, so nothing depends on one person's files.

We only have one standard.

iMS OS works for one standard or twenty. Start with one, and adding a second later reuses everything you've already mapped.

We don't have time to implement new software.

You don't start from scratch. Existing registers and documents are imported, and onboarding is guided step by step.

Is our data secure if auditors can see it?

Access is read-only by default, limited to the standards and evidence you choose, time-limited and logged. You decide who sees what and for how long.

Is AI safe to use for compliance?

Alice helps people find answers and evidence faster. Your team stays in control of decisions and records.
