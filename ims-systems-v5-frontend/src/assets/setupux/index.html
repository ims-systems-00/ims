      Why it works: it lives on the dashboard, not in a wizard

      The checklist is a card on the first screen, with a progress ring and the next step highlighted. People can leave and come back; nothing is locked behind it. "Hide for now" moves it to the sidebar as "Get set up 2/8".

      Guided steps happen on the real screen

      Pressing Continue opens the module and a small guide panel; a spotlight points at the exact button. Sub-steps tick as the user actually does them, and the empty states teach: "No units yet: create one, or ask Alice".

      Alice can do the step

      Every step that is data entry has "Ask Alice to do this": paste a list, drop a folder, choose toolkits. Alice previews what she will create; the user confirms. The dashboard fills as steps complete, so value shows early.
