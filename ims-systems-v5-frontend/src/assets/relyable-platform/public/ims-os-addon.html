Document Management

Upload a document

Ticking this sends the document's facts (type, issuer, reference, dates) to Relyable through the evidence SDK. The document itself never leaves iMS OS. Certificates with an issuer reference are verified with the issuer and count at Tier 2; expiry is tracked live.
