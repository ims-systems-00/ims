QR labels & scanning

Each asset gets a unique QR label. Scanning it on any phone opens the asset's own page: status, safety notes, last and next checks, and the actions the scanner may take.

Print labels

Durable polyester labels, 50 × 25 mm or 70 × 35 mm. Print this sheet or send to the label printer.

What a scan can do

AnyoneRead-only: identity, OK-to-use status, next due, safety notes.
StaffReport a fault, pre-use check, sighting, move location.
Competent person / contractorRecord inspection or calibration result with certificate; next due set automatically.
Auditor (remote audit link)Full history and certificates, read-only.

Disposals & retirements
